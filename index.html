1. Introduction

Nasaq helps businesses manage customer inquiries, maintain conversation context, and provide chatbot and sales-support functionality. This policy covers information handled through the Nasaq platform and its connected communication channels.

When you message a business using Nasaq, that business determines how it uses your information for its customer relationship. Nasaq processes conversation information to provide services to that business. The business may have its own privacy policy, which also applies to its activities.

2. Information We Collect

  • Business account information: business names, contact details, account and workspace identifiers, authorized user information, and business information supplied to configure the service, such as products, services, FAQs and policies.
  • Customer phone numbers and identifiers: WhatsApp phone numbers or channel identifiers, conversation and message identifiers, and identifiers needed to associate an inquiry with the correct business.
  • Messages and conversation content: information you send through connected channels, conversation history, timestamps, and responses, summaries or review records created to support your inquiry.
  • Technical and log information: connection and request metadata, IP addresses where available, delivery or processing status, errors, security records, and service usage or quota information.

Please avoid including passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

3. How We Use Information

We use information as needed to:

  • Provide and maintain chatbot and sales-support features for businesses.
  • Receive, route and process customer inquiries, prepare responses, and support review by authorized business staff.
  • Maintain conversation continuity and relevant context across messages.
  • Associate information with the correct business account, prevent duplicate processing, and manage service access and usage limits.
  • Protect accounts and systems, investigate errors or abuse, and improve service reliability and functionality.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

4. WhatsApp / Meta Data

Nasaq may process data received through the WhatsApp Business Platform, including customer identifiers, messages, timestamps and webhook event information. This processing is limited to providing the requested Nasaq services, supporting the relevant business conversations, and protecting those services.

Meta and WhatsApp also process information under their own terms and privacy policies. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy for information about WhatsApp's practices. Connecting WhatsApp does not make Nasaq part of Meta.

5. AI Processing

Conversation content and relevant business context may be processed by AI systems when needed to provide enabled Nasaq features, such as identifying the purpose of an inquiry, preparing a response, summarizing a conversation, or supporting sales qualification and follow-up.

Where an external AI service is used, relevant information may be shared with that service to perform the requested processing. AI-generated results can be inaccurate and may be referred to an authorized person for review. The information processed depends on the features the business uses.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Information may be made available to the business you contact and its authorized users so they can handle your inquiry. We share information with service and infrastructure providers only where necessary to operate Nasaq, such as hosting, storage, communications, security and AI processing providers. We limit sharing to what is needed for the relevant service.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or where necessary to protect the security and legal rights of users and the service. Providers may process information in countries other than yours; applicable protections depend on the service and location involved.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services and meet applicable security, operational and legal needs. Retention depends on the type of information, the business's service instructions, the account relationship, and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it non-identifying where appropriate. Backup copies may remain for their backup lifecycle, and limited records may be retained where required for legal obligations, resolving disputes, or preventing abuse. You may contact us to request deletion or information about retention for your situation.

8. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls, separation of business account data, secure credential handling, and protection of network connections. Access is limited according to the needs of the service.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe information associated with Nasaq has been compromised, please contact us.

9. User Rights / Data Deletion Requests

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, obtain a copy, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request, email malekhassonah@gmail.com with the subject Nasaq privacy request or Nasaq data deletion request. Explain your request and identify the business you contacted and the phone number or account identifier associated with the conversation, so we can locate the relevant records.

We may request proportionate information to verify your identity and protect others' data. Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys or payment-card details. If the business controls the information concerned, you can also contact it directly; we may coordinate your request with that business. We respond in accordance with applicable requirements and explain any lawful limitations on deletion or other requests.

10. Children

Nasaq is intended for businesses and their customer-service activities and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Nasaq, contact us so we can assess the situation and take appropriate action, including deletion where appropriate.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as Nasaq's services or information practices change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the Last Updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
